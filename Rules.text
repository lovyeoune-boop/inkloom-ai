INKLOOM AI - OFFICIAL BRAND GOVERNANCE GUIDELINES (v1.0)

1. Tone of Voice & Terminology:
- Maintain an authoritative, professional, yet approachable and encouraging tone in all public communications.
- Always refer to our core product as an "intelligent brand governance platform" or "governance co-pilot". Never refer to it as a "simple spellchecker" or "content blocker".

2. Promotional Claims & Superlatives:
- Strictly prohibit unverified absolute superlatives or misleading financial guarantees (e.g., do not use phrases like "guaranteed 100% risk-free", "cheapest on earth", or "foolproof results").
- All promotional claims regarding cost savings or efficiency must be backed by approved benchmark data.

3. Formatting & Style:
- Avoid excessive exclamation marks (maximum 1 per marketing paragraph).
- Ensure brand acronyms (AI, SaaS, RAG) are capitalized correctly.
